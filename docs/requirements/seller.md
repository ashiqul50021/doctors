# 📦 Module Requirements: Seller Portal (Ecommerce)

## Overview
This document specifies the functional requirements, workflows, and specifications for the Seller Portal within the E-commerce module of the Doccure platform.

---

## 1. Product Management

### 1.1 Product Creation & Specification
- **Product Classification (General vs Medical):**
  - **General Product:** Normal ecommerce items (Personal Care, Wellness, Fitness Items, Accessories). Standard title, category, price, gallery, and description.
  - **Medical / Healthcare Product:** Prescription drugs & specialized medical equipment. Includes additional fields: Generic Name, Manufacturer/Brand, Prescription Required Flag (`Yes`/`No`), Side Effects / Warnings.
- **Custom Landing Page / Custom Details Sections:**
  - Dynamic Custom Content Builder for Product Details Page.
  - Feature Blocks / Highlighting Banners (Custom images, video embeds).
  - FAQ / Accordion Section (Product-specific Q&A).
  - Key Ingredients / Usage Guide / How to Use Steps.
  - Specifications / Comparison Tables.
- **Basic Information:**
  - Product Title & Subtitle
  - Categories (Main Category, Sub-category, Child Category)
  - Short & Detailed Description (WYSIWYG Editor)
  - Feature Image (Thumbnail) & Gallery Images (Multiple image uploads)
  - Product Tags & Keywords for Search Optimization
- **Pricing & Tax:**
  - Regular Price (MSRP)
  - Discounted / Sale Price with optional sale date range
  - Cost Price (Internal calculation for profit margins)
- **Product Types & Variations:**
  - **Single Product:** Fixed pricing, unique SKU, and uniform stock count.
  - **Variant Product:** Multi-attribute support (e.g., Pack Size: 10 Strips, 1 Box; Volume: 100ml, 250ml; Power: 500mg, 1000mg). Each variant maintains its own SKU, Barcode, Price, and Stock level.

### 1.2 Admin Approval Workflow
- **Submission:** Newly created or heavily modified products enter the `Pending Approval` state by default.
- **Admin Moderation:**
  - **Approved:** Product status shifts to `Live` and becomes visible in the marketplace.
  - **Rejected:** Product status updates to `Rejected`. Admin provides a rejection reason/feedback. Seller can rectify and resubmit.
- **Product Lifecycle Statuses:**
  - `Draft`: Saved locally by seller, not yet submitted.
  - `Pending`: Awaiting administrator review.
  - `Approved / Live`: Active on public marketplace.
  - `Rejected`: Denied by admin with feedback notes.
  - `Disabled`: Temporarily taken offline by seller or admin.

### 1.3 Inventory & Stock Adjustment
- **Single Product Stock:**
  - Manual stock updates (Add / Deduct quantity).
  - Low Stock Threshold Alert (Notification when inventory falls below limit).
  - Stock Status (`In Stock`, `Out of Stock`, `Backorder`).
- **Variant Stock Management:**
  - Independent stock adjustment per SKU/Variant.
- **Stock Audit Log:**
  - Historical log recording stock change events (Manual adjustment, Order fulfillment, Return/Restock).

## 2. Order Management

### 2.1 Order Tracking & Filtering
- **Seller-Specific View:** Sellers can only view orders containing their respective products.
- **Filtering & Search:** Search by Order ID, Customer Name, Date Range, and Status (`Pending`, `Processing`, `Shipped`, `Delivered`, `Cancelled`, `Returned`).
- **Order Breakdown:**
  - Customer Shipping & Billing Address
  - Items ordered, variant specs, quantity, and unit prices
  - Payment Method & Payment Status (`Paid`, `Unpaid`)

### 2.2 Order Processing & Fulfillment
- **Fulfillment Status Transition:**
  - Admin updates order fulfillment status on behalf of seller or logistics: `Pending` ➔ `Processing` ➔ `Shipped / Dispatched` ➔ `Delivered`.
### 2.3 Product Reviews & Ratings Management
- **Customer Reviews View:** Sellers can view all ratings, written reviews, and uploaded review images for their products.
- **Open Review Submission:** Any user/visitor can submit a review and rating.
- **Admin Review Moderation:** All submitted reviews enter `Pending Approval` and require Admin approval before appearing on the product page.
- **Admin Custom Reviews:** Admins can manually create and publish custom reviews (with custom reviewer name, rating, comment, and date).
- **Seller Response Logic:** Sellers can post an official response/reply to approved reviews.
- **Seller Flagging:** Sellers can flag inappropriate reviews to Admin for deletion.
- **Review Analytics:** Average rating calculation per product and rating distribution breakdown (1-star to 5-star).

## 3. Financials & Payout System

### 3.1 Balance & Earnings Overview
- **Total Sales Revenue:** Gross revenue generated by seller products.
- **Platform Commission:** Deducted platform fee based on agreed percentage or fixed rates.
- **Available Balance:** Cleared funds available for immediate withdrawal.
- **Pending Balance:** Funds locked during the active order return/refund window.

### 3.2 Transaction History
- Itemized financial ledger for every completed order:
  - `Order ID`
  - `Item Sold & Quantity`
  - `Gross Amount`
  - `Admin Commission Deducted`
  - `Net Seller Profit`
  - `Timestamp`

### 3.3 Payout Method Configuration
Sellers can register and manage payout destination methods:
- **Bank Account Transfer:** Bank Name, Branch Name, Routing Number, Account Holder Name, Account Number.
- **Mobile Financial Services (MFS):** bKash, Nagad, Rocket (Phone Number, Account Type: Personal/Agent/Merchant).

### 3.4 Payout Request Workflow
- **Minimum Withdrawal Limit:** Withdrawal button unlocks when `Available Balance` >= Minimum Threshold (e.g., ৳1,000).
- **Request Submission:** Seller specifies withdrawal amount and selects active payout method.
- **Payout Request Statuses:**
  - `Pending`: Submitted and queued for admin review.
  - `Processing`: Disbursement underway.
  - `Paid / Completed`: Funds disbursed and transaction hash/reference attached.
  - `Rejected`: Request declined with explanation.
